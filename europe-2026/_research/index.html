What makes short video go viral

Research round 2 (27 Oct 2026): ~20 research papers, 2025–26 platform + creator guidance, and ~80 open-source tools and asset libraries with their licences checked. Applied to our Europe-trip series. Back to the reels hub.

1Top takeaways

5 sdecides almost everything

In ~90k Snapchat videos, staying past second 5 correlates 0.93 with total watch share. The joke must land by ~3 s.

⚡↗Surprise, then joy in pulses

Surprise holds attention; joy works by rising. Peak–valley–peak beats one long high (Teixeira et al., JMR).

1sting per joke, on the reaction

Place a sound on recognition/release, after a tiny gap. Bright, harsh audio lowered likes and comments.

🙈Don't caption the punchline early

A caption that shows the punch word before it's said kills the laugh. Split lines so it appears on time.

🔗Link every episode

Linked Reels (Aug 2025) put a "Watch Part" button on each reel; a long static end card invites a swipe.

📨Design for sends

Indians share more than most regions (Mosseri). Name who to send it to; ask real questions, not "comment 🔥".

👥Faces, close up, first

Wide shots opened 1% of viral TikToks vs 17% of non-viral. Scenery after the hook.

⚠️Civic-sense risk

Indian groups performing in public abroad drew pile-ons in 2026. Keep group singing self-deprecating; no bothered locals in shot.

6Licence traps

Meme originals

Not cleared, and sometimes mislabelled CC0 on Freesound. Synthesise a vine-boom-style hit instead.

CC BY needs a credit

Noto Animated, Twemoji, Q-DDL LUTs: add a credits line to the post caption.

No redistribution

Sonniss, Mixkit, Pixabay, LottieFiles: never publish the asset folder (our repo is private — keep it that way).

Model weights ≠ code

Prefer Qwen3-VL (Apache-2.0); some smaller Qwen and LLaMA-based models are non-commercial.
