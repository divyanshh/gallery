What makes travel photos perform on Instagram

Research for the trip's photo posts (27 Oct 2026): 22 research papers, 2025–26 Instagram guidance, and open-source editing tools and assets with their licences checked. Turned into rules I follow when editing your photos.

1Top takeaways

9×Carousels, not single photos

Carousels get ~9× the saves and ~2× the reach of single images; single-image reach fell 22% in 2026 (Metricool, 24M posts).

1 + 2Slide 1 and slide 2 are both covers

The cover dominates (+11% from layout alone in Airbnb data), and Instagram re-shows unswiped carousels starting at slide 2.

±Filters depend on the subject

Filtered people +27–68%, food +16%; filtered landscapes −18–36% (4.9M Flickr photos).

🔥Warm, never cold

Warmth, exposure, contrast up = best; red ×1.6 repins; blue, green, black-and-white hurt.

🧍People, the right way

Faces +38% likes; at landmarks a back-view or small-in-frame person raises appeal; strangers' faces lower it.

−31%Text off the photos

Images with text got 31% fewer views. Text goes on the cover hook and dedicated tip/cost slides only.

✨Raw beats polished

Mosseri: flawless looks read as AI. No sky swaps, no heavy beauty filters; AI labels cost ~7–8% likes.

📍Location + keywords

Posts are indexed by Google and shown on Instagram Map: exact location tag, place keywords in line 1, alt text.

3Carousel craft

Order

Best frame → second-best → story (arrive, landmark, detail, food, people, payoff) → tip/cost text slide → closing "Day N+1 next".

Length

6–10 slides; photo dumps up to 20 (curated, not random). All slides take slide 1's ratio.

Music

Add a track: carousels with music can show in the Reels tab (it mutes videos inside the carousel).

After posting

You can reorder slides and change the cover in the first hours — swap a weak cover.

Mix in video

A 3–6 s clip in the middle makes people stop and rewatch.

Originality

Own photos only; reposts and watermarked screenshots lose recommendations (Apr 2026).

5Instagram 2025–26

Photos aren't dead

Mosseri: photos aren't penalised; video just earns more recommendations. Carousels win saves.

3:4 grid

Since Jan 2025 the profile grid is 3:4. 1080×1440 matches the tile; 4:5 is the safe feed default.

20 slides

Any photo/video mix; schedulers using the API stop at 10.

Per-slide captions

Rolled out Jun 2026 — nice for tips, "not a reach hack".

Instagram Map

Location-tagged public posts appear on the Map (India too).

5 hashtags

Cap applies to photos and carousels too (Dec 2025).

Devanagari text

In-app text styling supports Hindi script (Nov 2025).

No photo trials yet

Trial posts exist for Reels only; Mosseri said photo trials "should" come.

8Licence traps

rembg default model

Now bria-rmbg — paid for commercial use. Always pick birefnet-general.

"Kodak / Fuji filter"

Trademarks. Call it "Portra-inspired" in public copy.

OSM maps

The credit must be inside the image, not just in the caption.

Free LUT sites

Often missing redistribution terms; check the licence inside the zip, never re-host.

10Export spec

Size1080×1350 (4:5) default · 1080×1440 (3:4) if every slide is 3:4
Grid cropCentre 3:4 shows on your profile — keep faces and text central
ColoursRGB with profile; convert iPhone HEIC / Display-P3 first
FileJPEG quality 90–92, 4:4:4, under 8 MB
SharpenLanczos resize, then a light sharpen
PrivacyGPS stripped from exported files unless you want it
AppSettings → Media quality → "Upload at highest quality"
Stories1080×1920, ~250 px clear top and bottom
